# Exemplars

The owner drops 5 to 10 of his own texts in this folder: past posts, talk transcripts or scripts he wrote himself and is happy with. These are the reference for the voice. They are read when a script is written, and they are the first thing to check when the voice drifts.

What belongs here:
- Plain text or Markdown files, one text per file.
- Only texts the owner wrote, or texts he has approved for this use.

What does not belong here:
- Other people's writing, including quotes from sources. Sources go in the claims, not in exemplars.
- Anything with employer, client or unreleased-deal details. Strip them before saving.
- Generated scripts. A generated script is not an exemplar until the owner has rewritten it and marked it as his own.

Until the owner adds files, the voice rules in `../voice-spec.md` are the only reference.
